This Privacy Policy applies to Institute 360, a product of NuTech Computers, Porur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600116. By using our platform, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Introduction & Scope

Institute 360 ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the personal data of institutes, administrators, faculty, and students who use our platform. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights with respect to your data.

This policy applies to all services provided through institute360.com and any associated mobile or web applications operated by NuTech Computers.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Institute Details: Name, address, type (school/college/university), number of students.
  • Administrator Details: Name, work email, phone number, designation.
  • Demo Request Data: Contact name, email, phone, and institute information submitted via our website form.

2.2 Student & Faculty Data (when platform is active)

  • Student enrollment records, attendance logs, academic performance data.
  • Faculty schedules, performance metrics, and course assignments.
  • Fee payment records and invoice history.

2.3 Automatically Collected Data

  • Browser type, IP address, device type, and operating system.
  • Pages visited, session duration, and usage patterns on our platform.
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see our Cookie Policy).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver and maintain the Institute 360 platform and its features.
  • To respond to demo requests and onboard new institutes.
  • To send service notifications, updates, and support communications.
  • To generate analytics and improve platform performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations under applicable Indian law (including the DPDP Act, 2023).
  • To process fee payments and generate invoices.
  • To prevent fraud and ensure platform security.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. We may share limited data with:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Partners: We use Neon (PostgreSQL database) and Render (cloud hosting) to operate our backend. These providers are bound by data processing agreements.
  • Payment Processors: When processing fee payments, data is shared with authorised payment gateways compliant with PCI-DSS standards.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law, court order, or government directive under applicable Indian regulations.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers. We implement the following safeguards:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Encrypted databases with restricted access controls.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
  • Role-based access control — only authorised personnel can access institute data.
  • Automatic backups to prevent data loss.

While we take all reasonable precautions, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure. We encourage users to use strong passwords and report any suspected breaches immediately to adminnutech@gmail.com.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your institute account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:

  • Demo request data: Retained for up to 12 months after last contact.
  • Active institute data: Retained for the duration of your subscription plus 1 year.
  • Financial records: Retained for 7 years as required by Indian accounting law.
  • Server logs: Retained for up to 90 days.

You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 7 below).

7. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: File a complaint with us if you believe your data rights have been violated.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate a person to exercise your rights in case of incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at adminnutech@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Rights Request".

8. Children's Data

Our platform processes student data on behalf of registered institutes. Student records are managed solely by authorised institute administrators. We do not directly collect personal data from children under 18 without consent from the respective institution acting in loco parentis.

Institutes using Institute 360 are responsible for ensuring appropriate consent mechanisms are in place for student data as required by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
  • Notify registered institutes via email or in-platform notification.

Continued use of Institute 360 after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
